Summary

01 / profile

Naveen has spent his career inside Odoo — building the modules that let a diocese manage its parishes, a manufacturer track its factory floor, or a retailer sync inventory across shops and an e-commerce site. He works across the stack: Python and Django on the server, PostgreSQL and MySQL underneath, Qweb for the reports finance teams live in, and enough JavaScript and HTML to own the front end too.

What sets him apart is the leadership layer on top of that: on Higrade+, a campus management system for Don Bosco College, he ran a ten-person team through two major Odoo versions. On most other projects he's the engineer clients and colleagues turn to when requirements are unclear and the system has to be designed from scratch.
